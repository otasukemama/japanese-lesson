Conversation Practice

Point at an object and ask "What is this?" — swap in a different word for the answer each time.

A
これはなんですか？
Kore wa nan desu ka?
What is this?
B
それは＿＿です。
Sore wa ___ desu.
That is ___.
A
それはなんですか？
Sore wa nan desu ka?
What is that (near you)?
B
これは＿＿です。
Kore wa ___ desu.
This is ___.
A
あれはなんですか？
Are wa nan desu ka?
What is that / it (over there)?
B
あれは＿＿です。
Are wa ___ desu.
That is ___.

A Note on Articles

Japanese has no words for "a," "an," or "the." これはペンです simply means "This is (a) pen" — no article needed, in either direction.
