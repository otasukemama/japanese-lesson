Katakana Reference Chart

Katakana is mainly used for loanwords and sound-effect words. The shapes differ from hiragana, but the sounds are exactly the same.

Basic Sounds (せいおん)

Pronounced exactly the same as hiragana. Let's get used to the different shapes first.

Tips for telling similar shapes apart
  • シ/ツ and ソ/ン look alike — check the stroke direction to tell them apart
  • ン (n) is its own sound and doesn't belong to any row or column
