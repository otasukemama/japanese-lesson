すうじ — Numbers 1–100

1〜10 are the building blocks. Everything else is made by combining them.

How to combine them
  • 11–19: じゅう + the digit — 11 = じゅういち, 15 = じゅうご
  • 21–29 etc: the tens word + the digit — 21 = にじゅういち, 45 = よんじゅうご
  • 4 and 7 usually use よん／なな in counting (not し／しち), to avoid confusion with 死(death)／質

Quick guide
  • つ: a handy all-purpose counter — use this if you're not sure
  • こ: small, roundish things like apples, bread, erasers
  • にん: counting people. Only 1 and 2 have special readings
  • ほん: long, thin things like pens, umbrellas, bottles, bananas
  • まい: flat, thin things like paper, shirts, plates

Quick guide
  • ひき: small-to-medium animals like cats, dogs, fish (large animals use とう instead)
  • だい: cars, TVs, computers — machines and vehicles
  • さつ: books, notebooks, magazines
  • はい: coffee, water, sake — anything in a cup or glass
  • かい is only for counting occurrences: いっかい (once), にかい (twice), さんかい (three times)…

Examples

りんごが みっつ あります。
Ringo ga mittsu arimasu.
There are three apples.
ねこが にひき います。
Neko ga nihiki imasu.
There are two cats.
かぞくは よにんです。
Kazoku wa yonin desu.
There are four people in my family.
コーヒーを いっぱい ください。
Ko-hi- wo ippai kudasai.
One coffee, please.
Usage tip

Don't try to memorize everything at once — start with what the student will actually use in conversation (a cup of coffee, family size) and build from there.
