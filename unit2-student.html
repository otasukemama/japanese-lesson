Unit 2: Self-Introduction
ユニット2：じこしょうかい

Goal: introduce yourself and ask where someone is from — in one flowing conversation.

STEP 1

Learn today's words

Listen to your teacher, then repeat each word out loud.

🙋
わたし
watashi
I / me
📛
なまえ
namae
name
🌏
くに
kuni
country
🇯🇵
にほん
nihon
Japan
🤝
はじめまして
hajimemashite
nice to meet you
🙇
よろしく
おねがいします
yoroshiku onegaishimasu
let's get along (polite)
STEP 2

Reading practice

Look at the hiragana chart and read the 6 words above out loud.

STEP 3

Conversation practice: meet someone new

First, learn two useful questions. Then put everything together in one conversation.

🗺️
どこから
きましたか？
doko kara kimashita ka?
Where are you from?
👉
〜さんは？
~ san wa?
And you, ~?

Unit 2 Summary（ユニット2のまとめ）

  • I can introduce myself with my name.
  • I can say where I'm from.
  • I can ask "Where are you from?" and answer.
  • I can read today's words using the hiragana chart.
