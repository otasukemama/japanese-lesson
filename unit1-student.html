A Note on "ございます"

The ございます part makes a phrase more polite — used with people you don't know well, elders, or in shops (おはようございます, ありがとうございます). With your own kids or close family, it's natural to drop it and just say おはよう or ありがとう instead.

Reading Practice

Look at the hiragana chart and sound out each word above, letter by letter. Then read the whole word smoothly.

Conversation Practice

Practice each pattern with different names — your own, a family member's, a friend's. Say it many times until it feels natural.
