Unit 0: Sound Reading Warm-up
ユニット0：おとをよむ

A short warm-up before Unit 1 — read the basic sounds, then practice a few simple words.

The Basic Character Chart（五十音表）

This is the basic character chart — think of it as the Japanese version of the English alphabet. The letters across the top (a, k, s…) show the consonants. The letters down the left side (a, i, u, e, o) show the vowels. Each sound is a consonant + a vowel. (The first column, あ い う え お, is vowels only.) For example: k + a = ka (か).

STEP 1

Repeat after the teacher

Listen to your teacher, then repeat each sound out loud.

STEP 2

Read the sounds your teacher points to

Your teacher will point to sounds at random. Say each one out loud.

STEP 3

Read simple words

Now put the sounds together into real words. Point to each letter on the chart above as you read.

STEP 4

There are more characters!

Besides the 46 basic sounds, there are other hiragana characters. You don't need to memorize them now — just knowing they exist is enough for this stage.

📌 No need to memorize yet — this applies to all the charts in Step 4 (voiced sounds, combined sounds, and special sounds).

Voiced Sounds (だくおん・はんだくおん)No need to memorize yet

Adding ゛ or ゜ to the か・さ・た・は rows changes the sound. Just read through once or twice.

gzdbp

Combined Sounds (ようおん)No need to memorize yet

A small ゃ・ゅ・ょ after an "i" sound blends into one syllable, like き + small ゃ = きゃ.

kya-rowsha-rowcha-rownya-rowhya-rowmya-rowrya-rowgya-rowja-rowbya-rowpya-row

Unit 0 Summary（ユニット0のまとめ）

  • I can read the basic hiragana sounds (五十音).
  • I can read simple words while looking at the chart.
  • I know there are other hiragana characters too.
